# Research

This directory contains the evidence used to design UX MeasureLab.

## Rules

- Preserve original sources in `source/`.
- Record claims separately from product recommendations.
- Prefer primary research, official product documentation, and regulator guidance.
- Mark community reports and vendor marketing as lower-confidence evidence.
- Do not convert a repeated practice into a universal threshold without validation.

## Current source

- `source/deep-research-summary.pdf`: a 13-page research summary compiled from public analytics and UX sources.

The summary is useful for concept formation but its line-style citation markers do not include a readable bibliography. Claims requiring implementation, statistical, legal, or vendor decisions remain provisional until their primary sources are registered.
